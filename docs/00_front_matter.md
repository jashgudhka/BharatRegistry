## Executive Summary

The **Bharat Registry** is a sovereign-grade digital transformation project designed to migrate India's land governance from a "Presumptive" paradigm to a "Conclusive" cryptographic certainty. By leveraging **Hyperledger Besu** and the **IBFT 2.0 consensus protocol**, we have engineered a multi-layered distributed ledger system that eliminates the "Mutation-Registration Gap," neutralizes the risk of double-selling, and unlocks "Dead Capital" through decentralized asset tokenization. This investigation details the full-stack engineering of the registry, from Solidity smart contracts to IPFS-based document sharding, providing a blueprint for a truly **Digital India**.
